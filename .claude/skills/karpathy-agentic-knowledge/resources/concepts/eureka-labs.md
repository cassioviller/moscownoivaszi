# Eureka Labs

Eureka Labs is Andrej Karpathy’s AI‑native education venture announced in July 2024. The goal is to build a learning platform that combines human teachers with AI assistants in a **teacher + AI symbiosis**.

## Vision

- **Ideal experience:** The best learning happens under passionate teachers who are infinitely patient and fluent in all languages. Eureka Labs aims to approximate this by pairing subject matter experts with AI teaching assistants that guide students through curated materials.
- **Expansion of reach and extent:** Karpathy envisions education that allows more people to learn more subjects, beyond what is possible today without assistance.

## First Product: LLM101n

- **LLM101n** is an undergraduate‑level course that teaches students to train their own AI models. It will be available online with both digital and physical cohorts.

## Implementation Notes for the Skill

- When creating educational content, pair human explanations with agentic tutorials and interactive experiments.
- Use `Program.md` loops to design courses: define roles (teacher agent, student agent), objectives, metrics and evaluation criteria.
- Store course materials in structured notes with examples, questions and projects.
