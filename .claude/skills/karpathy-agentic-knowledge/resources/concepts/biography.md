# Biography of Andrej Karpathy

## Early life and education

Andrej Karpathy was born on **23 October 1986 in Bratislava**, Czechoslovakia
(now Slovakia), and moved with his family to **Toronto, Canada, at age 15**.

- **BSc — University of Toronto (2005–2009).** Double major in Computer Science
  and Physics, with a minor in Mathematics.
- **MSc — University of British Columbia (2009–2011).** Worked on learning
  controllers for **physically-simulated figures** under adviser **Michiel van de
  Panne** (not Geoffrey Hinton).
- **PhD — Stanford University (2011–2016).** Advised by **Fei-Fei Li** at the
  Stanford Vision Lab; thesis *Connecting Images and Natural Language*. He
  created and was primary instructor for **CS231n: Convolutional Neural Networks
  for Visual Recognition**, one of the most influential deep-learning courses.

## Academic contributions

His early research pioneered visual–semantic alignment for image captioning
(Deep Visual–Semantic Alignments), dense captioning (DenseCap), and the
interpretability of recurrent networks (Visualizing and Understanding Recurrent
Networks). See `scientific-publications-map.md` for details.

## Industry roles

- **OpenAI (2015–2017, 2023–2024)** — Founding member and research scientist on
  early deep-learning/computer-vision work; returned in 2023 to work on a team
  focused on mid-training and synthetic data, contributing to GPT-4 / ChatGPT.
- **Tesla (2017–2022)** — Senior Director of AI; led the Autopilot vision team
  for large-scale data collection, neural-network training, and deployment of
  computer-vision models for Full Self-Driving.
- **Eureka Labs (2024)** — Founded an AI-native education company built around a
  "teacher + AI" model. Its first course is **LLM101n: Let's Build a
  Storyteller**, teaching students to train their own language models.
- **Anthropic (2026–present)** — Announced on **19 May 2026** that he joined
  Anthropic. He works on the **pre-training team** under team lead **Nick
  Joseph**, helping launch a team focused on using Claude itself to accelerate
  pre-training research. He noted he remains passionate about education and
  plans to return to it in time.

## Public influence and coinages

Karpathy is among the most widely followed AI educators and commentators. He
coined or popularized **vibe coding** (Feb 2025), later reframed as **agentic
engineering**, along with **Software 3.0**, **token throughput / tokenmaxxing**,
the **slopacolypse**, **jagged intelligence**, the **"decade of agents"**, and
the **march of nines** framing for reliability. He is the author of widely used
educational repositories including **micrograd**, **nanoGPT/nanochat**,
**llm.c**, and **AutoResearch**.
