## Tokenmaxxing trend (March–April 2026)

Articles and podcasts highlight a new workplace trend called **tokenmaxxing**, where engineers maximise AI token consumption as a productivity metric. Karpathy said he feels nervous when he has unused token budget and switches between tools to maximise usage. Commentators warn that tokenmaxxing can reward activity over outcomes and encourage waste. This event emphasises the need to measure **inference yield** (value per token) rather than raw token counts.
